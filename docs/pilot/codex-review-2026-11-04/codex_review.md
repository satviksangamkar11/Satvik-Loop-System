**Top 5 risks**

1. **Unapproved paid spending:** model overrides can bypass paid classification, while the queue’s declared paid budget is not the runtime ceiling (`S/stage_config.py:399`, `S/openrouter_call.py:868`, `monitor/actions.py:117`).
2. **Planner output can write outside its artifact directory:** validation occurs too late (`S/plan_job.py:273`, `monitor/actions.py:246`).
3. **STOP does not stop the production ladder between steps:** the callback is lost at dispatch (`S/fix_loop.py:975`, `S/fix_loop.py:1544`).
4. **A successful paid job may fail delivery immediately:** the committed merge environment does not satisfy the current suite; reviewed-byte integrity is also not re-established after rebase (`S/merge_queue.py:39`, `tools/sandbox/Dockerfile:9`, `S/merge_queue.py:93`).
5. **Crashes can lose scheduling or repeat effects:** both merge recovery and API idempotency lack a durable operation-state protocol (`S/merge_queue.py:66`, `monitor/store.py:100`).
